Questions

Common questions

Do you work with clients outside my area?

Yes. Everything runs online through secure document sharing and video calls. Confirm the states or countries you serve.

Which accounting software do you use?

QuickBooks Online and Xero. If you use something else, we can help you move over or work within it.

Do you file taxes?

Answer to confirm. If tax filing is not included, we prepare tax-ready books and coordinate directly with your CPA.

How long is the commitment?

Monthly plans run month to month. Strategy projects are scoped and priced up front.

What if my books are a mess?

That's a common starting point. We review where things stand and quote a one-time clean-up before monthly service begins.
